# Austin Energy Residential Rate Information

## Overview

Austin Energy is the publicly owned electric utility of Austin, Texas, serving more than 500,000 customers and over 1 million residents in the Greater Austin area.

## Residential Rate Structure (2024-2025)

### Tiered Energy Charges

Austin Energy uses a tiered rate structure for residential customers:

| Tier | Usage (kWh) | Summer Rate | Non-Summer Rate |
|------|-------------|-------------|-----------------|
| Tier 1 | 0-500 kWh | 3.626¢/kWh | 2.801¢/kWh |
| Tier 2 | 501-1000 kWh | 6.572¢/kWh | 5.747¢/kWh |
| Tier 3 | 1001-1500 kWh | 8.572¢/kWh | 7.747¢/kWh |
| Tier 4 | 1501-2500 kWh | 10.072¢/kWh | 9.247¢/kWh |
| Tier 5 | Over 2500 kWh | 11.572¢/kWh | 10.747¢/kWh |

### Summer vs Non-Summer Seasons

- **Summer Season**: June through September (4 months)
- **Non-Summer Season**: October through May (8 months)

### Customer Charge

All residential customers pay a fixed monthly customer charge:
- **Standard Residential**: $10.00/month
- This charge appears on your bill regardless of energy usage

### Additional Charges

1. **Power Supply Adjustment**: Variable charge that adjusts based on fuel costs
2. **Community Benefit Charge**: Supports community programs and efficiency initiatives
3. **Regulatory Charge**: Covers regulatory compliance costs

## Peak Hours Information

While Austin Energy does not currently have mandatory time-of-use rates for residential customers, they recommend reducing usage during peak demand periods:

- **Summer Peak Hours**: 3:00 PM - 7:00 PM weekdays
- **Winter Peak Hours**: 6:00 AM - 9:00 AM and 6:00 PM - 9:00 PM weekdays

During peak demand events, Austin Energy may issue conservation appeals asking customers to reduce usage.

## Energy Efficiency Programs

Austin Energy offers various programs to help customers reduce energy consumption:

1. **Home Performance with ENERGY STAR**: Whole-home energy improvements
2. **Free Home Energy Audit**: Assessment of energy efficiency opportunities
3. **Appliance Rebates**: Incentives for efficient appliances
4. **Solar Rebates**: Support for residential solar installation
