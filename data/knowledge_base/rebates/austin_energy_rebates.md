# Austin Energy Rebate Programs

## Heat Pump Water Heater Rebate

### Rebate Amount
**$800** for qualifying ENERGY STAR certified hybrid/heat pump water heaters

### Eligibility Requirements

1. The heat pump water heater must be installed in a home with permanent electric service from Austin Energy
2. If replacing an existing unit, it must be 10 years old or older
3. Must purchase a new qualifying ENERGY STAR certified hybrid/heat pump water heater
4. Unit must have an Energy Factor (EF) of 2.0 or better
5. Installation of gas water heaters is NOT eligible

### Application Requirements

- Submit application within **90 days** of purchase
- Applications submitted after 90 days will not be approved
- Must adhere to all applicable permitting requirements
- Property owners or electric account holders are eligible to receive rebates

### Why Choose Heat Pump Water Heaters?

Heat pump water heaters (hybrid electric water heaters) transfer heat instead of creating it, making them **3-4 times more efficient** than traditional water heaters.

Benefits:
- Lower operating costs
- Reduced energy consumption
- Dehumidification as a side benefit
- Long equipment lifespan

## Home Performance with ENERGY STAR

### Program Overview

A whole-house approach to energy efficiency that includes:
- Air sealing
- HVAC tune-ups or replacement
- Duct repair and sealing
- Insulation improvements
- Other weatherization measures

### Incentives

- **Average rebate**: Approximately $2,600-$3,000
- **Financing**: Low-interest loan option with subsidized rates as low as 1.99%
- **0% financing** available for qualified improvements

### Process

1. Schedule a home energy assessment
2. Receive recommendations for improvements
3. Choose a participating contractor
4. Complete improvements
5. Submit rebate application
6. Receive rebate payment

## Smart Thermostat Rebate

### Rebate Amount
**$85** for qualifying smart thermostats

### Eligible Products
- Ecobee
- Nest Learning Thermostat
- Honeywell Home T-Series
- Other ENERGY STAR certified smart thermostats

### Requirements
- Must have Austin Energy electric service
- One rebate per household
- Thermostat must be new (not refurbished)

## Insulation Rebates

### Attic Insulation
- **$0.20 per square foot** for attic insulation upgrades
- Minimum R-30 required for rebate
- Maximum rebate: $500

### Requirements
- Professional installation required
- Must improve insulation to at least R-30
- Submit invoice and proof of installation

---

## Solar Rebates

### Residential Solar Installation

Austin Energy has historically offered some of the most generous solar rebates in Texas, though programs change annually.

Current considerations:
- Net metering available for excess generation
- Value of Solar rate for credited electricity
- Federal tax credits (30% through 2032)

### Requirements
- System must be installed by approved contractor
- Interconnection agreement required
- System inspection required
